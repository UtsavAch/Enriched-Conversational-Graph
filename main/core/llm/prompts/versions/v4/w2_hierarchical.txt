You classify TOPIC-STRUCTURE relations between a new conversation turn and a set
of earlier turns.

For each candidate, choose exactly one label:

- subcase     - the NEW turn is MORE SPECIFIC than the candidate: it narrows the
                focus, adds detail, or treats a particular case of the
                candidate's broader topic.
- supercase   - the NEW turn is MORE GENERAL than the candidate: it broadens the
                scope, summarises it together with other turns, or turns the
                candidate's particular case into a general principle.
- same_level  - the two turns address parallel topics at a similar level of
                specificity (typically two sub-topics of the same broader topic).
- no_relation - no meaningful topical connection. This is the correct answer for
                most pairs. Do not force a relation.

DIRECTION IS LOAD-BEARING. The label describes the NEW turn relative to the
CANDIDATE. If the new turn asks about a detail of what the candidate discussed,
that is subcase (NEW is more specific), not supercase.

LINK TO THE IMMEDIATE PARENT ONLY. Topics form a tree. When the new turn narrows
a topic, mark subcase ONLY against the most specific candidate it narrows (its
direct parent). Do NOT also mark subcase against that parent's own parent or
earlier ancestors: those links already exist through the parent, and repeating
them is the most common error in this task. Most turns have at most one subcase
edge.

SUPERCASE NEEDS AN ACTUAL GENERALISING MOVE: the new turn summarises or recaps
the candidate (usually together with other turns), or lifts the candidate's
specific solution into a general rule. A new turn that merely uses broader words,
or a new question on a different subject, is not supercase.

Emit no_relation freely. A graph where everything connects to everything carries
no information.

Note: the new turn's answer, and any candidate's answer, may contain bracketed
markers like [N_3]. These are citation/provenance markers — classify against the
candidate list given below, never against an ID that only appears inside bracket
text.

Return strict JSON only, no preamble, no markdown fences:
{{"<candidate_id>": "<label>", ...}}

Include every candidate id in the output. Use "no_relation" for pairs with no
meaningful topical connection.

FEW-SHOT EXAMPLE (immediate parent only):

NEW:
Q: "For the watering rota you just set up - should the Saturday slot be two
    people instead of one in July?"
A: "Yes, in July the Saturday slot needs two: the beds dry out fastest then."

CANDIDATES:
- [N_1] The garden association sets its goal: run the community garden this season.
- [N_4] Volunteers will share watering through a weekly rota.
- [N_6] The rota assigns one volunteer per day, with Saturdays covered by Ana.

→ {{"N_1": "no_relation", "N_4": "no_relation", "N_6": "subcase"}}
(NEW narrows N_6's rota detail, so it is subcase of N_6 only. N_4 and N_1 are
N_6's ancestors in the topic tree: the path NEW -> N_6 -> N_4 -> N_1 already
connects them, so they get no_relation here, not subcase.)

FEW-SHOT EXAMPLE (supercase as a recap):

NEW:
Q: "Before the meeting, can you sum up what we've agreed for the garden so far?"
A: "Watering runs on a weekly rota, compost goes in the north corner, and the
    seed budget is capped at €300."

CANDIDATES:
- [N_4] Volunteers will share watering through a weekly rota.
- [N_9] The compost bins go in the north corner, away from the beds.
- [N_12] The seed budget is capped at €300 for the season.
- [N_13] Asks which tomato variety grows best in partial shade.

→ {{"N_4": "supercase", "N_9": "supercase", "N_12": "supercase", "N_13": "no_relation"}}
(NEW summarises three earlier decisions, so it is more general than each. N_13
is not part of the summary.)

FEW-SHOT EXAMPLE (supercase without an explicit recap):

NEW:
Q: "We'll also need rotas for weeding and for the tool shed keys. Should each get
    its own spreadsheet like the watering one?"
A: "Better to make one shared volunteer rota with a task column (watering,
    weeding, keys), so availability is tracked in a single place instead of
    three."

CANDIDATES:
- [N_6] The rota assigns one volunteer per day, with Saturdays covered by Ana.

→ {{"N_6": "supercase"}}
(N_6 solved ONE case - watering. NEW lifts that solution into a general scheme
for every task. Nothing announces a summary; the generalising move is implicit
in what the turn does. That is still supercase.)

FEW-SHOT EXAMPLE (same_level):

NEW:
Q: "Separately - how should we handle the membership fee this year?"
A: "Keep it at €20, payable at the first meeting."

CANDIDATES:
- [N_4] Volunteers will share watering through a weekly rota.
